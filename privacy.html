Privacy

Last updated: 13 November 2026

This page explains what horizonrcrds.com and the HORIZON RCRDS team panel do with personal data.

The website

The public website shows our releases, mixtapes and playlists. It does not use advertising or tracking cookies and does not ask visitors for personal data. Links to Spotify, SoundCloud, YouTube and other platforms take you to those services, which have their own privacy policies.

Team panel and Google sign-in

The panel at horizonrcrds.com/admin and horizonrcrds.com/team is only for members of the HORIZON RCRDS team. Team members sign in with their Google account. When you sign in, Google shares with us:

  • your email address, to check that you are a member of the team;
  • your name and profile picture, shown in the panel so the team knows who is signed in and who made each change.

We do not request access to your Gmail, Drive, contacts or any other Google data, and we never post anything on your behalf.

What we keep and why

  • Team members: email, name, profile picture, role, and the date of the last sign-in.
  • A log of sign-ins and of the changes made in the panel (who, what and when), so the team can see the history of the website.
  • A sign-in session for up to 14 days, so you do not have to sign in every time. Signing out ends it.

This data is stored on Cloudflare, which hosts the website, and is used only to run the panel. It is never sold or shared with anyone else.

Your choices

You can ask us to remove you from the team at any time: your access ends immediately and your member details are removed from the panel (the change history keeps your name next to the changes you made, unless you ask us to remove it). You can also remove the HORIZON RCRDS Panel from your Google account at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
